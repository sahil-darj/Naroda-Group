Project Overview

Welcome to Naroda Arise

A premium lifestyle project by Gopalanand Corporation offering luxurious living and retail shops.

Modern Living with Timeless Elegance

Naroda Arise represents a new benchmark in sophisticated living. Developed by Gopalanand Corporation, this project seamlessly integrates luxurious 2 & 3 BHK residences with vibrant retail shops, ensuring everything you need is right at your doorstep.

With Italian marble print tiles, granite kitchen platforms, and premium brand fittings, each home is crafted to provide a sanctuary of comfort. The earthquake-resistant RCC frame and 24/7 security offer complete peace of mind for you and your family.
